Delete your My Dear Partner account

You can request account deletion in the app or contact our support team if you cannot access the app.

In the app

  1. Sign in to My Dear Partner and open Settings.
  2. Open Delete Account and read the confirmation.
  3. Confirm and select Request Deletion.

A confirmed request deactivates your account and schedules deletion. The app displays your recovery deadline. You can recover your account within the 30-day recovery window.

Without the app

Email support@mydearpartner.in with the subject “Account deletion request”. Include the email address or phone number associated with your account so support can identify it and explain how to verify ownership.

Do not send your password, payment card details or one-time codes. Sending an email does not itself confirm or complete deletion. Support will confirm receipt, ownership verification and the applicable timeline.

Data covered

Your request covers account and profile information, photos, verification uploads, messages and device notification tokens associated with your account. Limited payment, fraud prevention, safety or legally required records may need to be retained. Contact support for the applicable retention periods, backup handling and completion status. Copies already saved by other members may remain with them.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Account deletion does not automatically cancel an independently recurring payment arrangement.
