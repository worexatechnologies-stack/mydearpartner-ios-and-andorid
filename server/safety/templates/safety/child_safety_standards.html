Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy

App: My Dear Partner (MyDearPartner)  |  Developer: Worexa Technologies  |  Last Updated: October 2026
Zero Tolerance Notice: MyDearPartner strictly prohibits any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). Any account attempting to exploit minors or share illicit content will be immediately permanently banned and reported to law enforcement authorities.

1. Age Restrictions (Strictly 18+)

MyDearPartner is a matrimonial and matchmaking platform exclusively created for adults aged 18 years or older who are seeking marriage and life companionship. Minors (anyone under the age of 18) are strictly prohibited from creating an account, browsing profiles, or using this application. We enforce calendar date-of-birth age gating during registration.

2. Explicit Prohibition of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

In compliance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and international child protection laws, MyDearPartner enforces a zero-tolerance policy against:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Production, distribution, uploading, requesting, or transmission of visual, audio, or textual depictions of minors in sexually suggestive or explicit contexts.
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE): Grooming of minors, sexual extortion (sextortion), underage solicitations, child trafficking, or any sexualized interactions involving minors.
  • Synthetic & AI-Generated Minor Depictions: Generation, alteration, or sharing of synthetic or AI-generated sexually explicit depictions of children or adolescents.

3. Reporting & In-App Safety Tools

Users have access to direct reporting tools built into every profile and chat screen:

  • Report Profile: Tap the options menu on any profile and select "Report" → "Underage user" or "Child safety concern".
  • Report Message: Users can tap and hold or choose "Report" on individual incoming messages.
  • Immediate Block: Users can instantly block any individual to cease all communication.

4. Enforcement and Escalation

Reports categorized under child safety receive immediate priority review by our dedicated safety team:

  • Content is immediately sequestered and removed.
  • The offending user account and associated device identifiers are permanently banned.
  • In accordance with legal obligations, confirmed CSAM/CSAE incidents are escalated and reported to relevant national and international child protection authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement.

5. Designated Child Safety Contact Person / Team

If you have any child safety concerns, suspect an underage account, or need to report illegal activity, please contact our designated child safety officer directly:

Designated Safety Team: MyDearPartner Child Safety & Grievance Cell
Email: support@mydearpartner.in
Operating Entity: Worexa Technologies
Response Time: Immediate escalation within 24 hours
